TrickSEOEN

See your page the way Googlebot does.

30-second trick: See your page the way Googlebot does. Search Console's URL Inspection tool can fetch a live page as Google and show the rendered HTML, a screenshot, and the resources it could not load. Try it now: Search the rendered HTML for your main heading and your key facts. If they are missing there, Google may not see them either. Myth vs factSEOEN

Myth: a sitemap gets every page indexed.

Myth vs fact: Myth: a sitemap gets every page indexed. A sitemap helps search engines discover your pages. Google says plainly that it does not guarantee every listed page will be crawled and indexed. Check it: Open the Pages report in Search Console and filter it by your sitemap. GlossarySecurityEN

SRI: a fingerprint for borrowed scripts.

Word of the day: SRI: a fingerprint for borrowed scripts. Subresource Integrity puts a hash of the file in the tag. If the CDN ever serves anything else, the browser refuses to run it. Spot it: List the scripts you load from other domains. Add an integrity hash to every one that has a fixed version.
