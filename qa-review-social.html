GlossarySEOEN

hreflang: the right page for each language.

Word of the day: hreflang: the right page for each language. An attribute that tells search engines which language or region each version of a page is for, so a Spanish speaker lands on the Spanish page. Spot it: Open one translated page and check that it links to every language version, itself included. Google ignores links that are not returned. Myth vs factSecurityEN

Myth: the padlock means the site is secure.

Myth vs fact: Myth: the padlock means the site is secure. HTTPS encrypts the trip between the browser and the server. It says nothing about the page's scripts, headers and cookies, or about who is behind the site. Check it: Check what the padlock does not cover: response headers, cookie flags, outdated scripts and a DMARC record. Myth vs factAccessibilityEN▶ ES

Myth: a Lighthouse 100 means accessible.

Myth vs fact: Myth: a Lighthouse 100 means accessible. Lighthouse checks what software can detect, such as names, contrast and ARIA. It cannot tell whether alt text makes sense or whether a screen reader user can finish the task. Check it: After the score, try one real task with a screen reader: VoiceOver on a Mac, NVDA on Windows.
