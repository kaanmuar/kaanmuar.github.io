Pages declare their regional language, like es-CO, and carry rich JSON-LD. But the pages analyzed send no HSTS, no content security policy, no X-Frame-Options, and no nosniff, and http moves to https with a 302.

Lesson: Start with four headers: Strict-Transport-Security, X-Content-Type-Options, frame-ancestors, and a report-only CSP.

One automated analysis of public pages on 19 Oct 2026. A starting point, not a verdict.
