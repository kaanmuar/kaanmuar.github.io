UNESCO has llms.txt, but no HSTS.

UNESCO publishes an llms.txt for AI tools, sets a content security policy, and limits framing to itself. But no page analyzed sends Strict-Transport-Security, and http:// moves to https with a temporary 302. The lesson for every team: Send Strict-Transport-Security with a max-age of a year or more, and make the http-to-https hop a 301. One automated analysis of public pages on 19 Oct 2026. A starting point, not a verdict.
