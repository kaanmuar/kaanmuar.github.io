Preloaded HSTS, a Permissions-Policy, security.txt, and Core Web Vitals that pass for real visitors. But the international and UK front pages have no h1, so screen-reader users lose the first heading.

Lesson: Give every page one h1 that names it, even a visually hidden one on a front page. Headings are how many people navigate.
