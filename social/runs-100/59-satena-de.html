HSTS marked for preload, nosniff, framing limited to its origin, and a strict Referrer-Policy. But none of the 20 pages analyzed declares a canonical URL, so search engines guess which version to index.

Lesson: Add a self-referencing canonical to every page, plus hreflang links between the language versions.
