The zombie redirect chain: it keeps coming back.

An old link hops from http to https, then to www, then to a trailing slash. Every hop costs a round trip, and crawlers can stop following a long chain before they reach the page. Exorcise it today: Follow your old links to their final address. Send each one there in a single redirect, and update the links you control.
