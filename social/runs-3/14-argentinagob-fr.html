A meta description on every page analyzed, full share cards, a sitemap, and every internal link opened answered. But the home page head loads 28 scripts with no async or defer, and nothing paints until each one arrives.

Lesson: Add defer to scripts the first screen doesn't need. The page can paint while they load.
