YouTube
Pages show publish dates, some headings are phrased as questions, and the text reads without JavaScript. But the start page still loads analytics.js with two UA- properties, which Google stopped processing in 2024.

Lesson: Remove the old analytics.js tags: a request and cookies for data no one receives.
