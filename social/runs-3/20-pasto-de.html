Made-up paths get a real 404, the page text is in the HTML, and the start page declares Spanish for Colombia. But every page analyzed loads gtag.js twice, for two GA4 IDs, so one visit can land in two reports.

Lesson: Keep one GA4 property per site, loaded once, ideally through Tag Manager, and retire the old ID.
