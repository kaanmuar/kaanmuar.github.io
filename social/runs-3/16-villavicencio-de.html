Structured data that names the city hall, full share cards, one-year HSTS, and a permanent https redirect. But the home page's <head> loads 15 scripts without async or defer, and the HTML alone weighs 549 KB.

Lesson: Add defer to scripts that don't need to run before first paint, and move inline data out of the HTML.

One automated analysis of public pages on 21 Oct 2026. A starting point, not a verdict.
