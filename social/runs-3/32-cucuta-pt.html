YouTube
A sitemap index, structured data naming the city hall, full share cards, alt text on every image, and a permanent https redirect. But no Strict-Transport-Security header is sent, so browsers never remember it.

Lesson: Send Strict-Transport-Security with max-age=31536000 on every https response once all subdomains serve https.
