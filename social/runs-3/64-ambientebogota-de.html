One-year HSTS with preload, nosniff, a strict Referrer-Policy, and framing limited to its origin. But robots.txt names no sitemap and /sitemap.xml answers 404, so crawlers find pages by links alone.

Lesson: Publish a sitemap.xml of the pages you want found and name it in robots.txt with one Sitemap: line.

One automated analysis of public pages on 21 Oct 2026. A starting point, not a verdict.
