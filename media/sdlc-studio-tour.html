The SDLC studio is a free, in-browser sprint with four agents. Ticket Steward rewrites the story on Jira, Azure DevOps, Monday, Trello, Linear, Asana, MS Project, or Backlog. Test Designer writes Gherkin and Xray cases. Automation Engineer generates Playwright, Cypress, and Robot scripts. Release Verifier checks the Definition of Done. No account is needed.
