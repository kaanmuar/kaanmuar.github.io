QA-lab URL Review This service

One review of the whole public site

Checks Google Web Risk and Cloudflare URL Scanner; every third-party script, frame, form and link host; spam injected into search results, cloaking for Googlebot and hidden links; lookalike domains that have a website or mail server; free DNS blocklists such as SpamCop, Mailspike and NordSpam; SPF and DMARC in depth; and the DKIM keys at the selectors mail services use by default. Exposed files are probed only after you prove the site is yours. The WordPress check matches versions against the Wordfence Intelligence feed and switches on once our copy of the feed is stored. If a review can't finish right away, the full report is emailed as soon as it is ready, usually within 48 hours.

  • Phishing and malware lists beyond Google
  • Hacked-site signs: search spam, cloaking, hidden links
  • Lookalike domains imitating the site
  • Third-party hosts the site loads or links to
  • Exposed files such as .env, .git or backups
  • WordPress core, plugin and theme vulnerabilities
  • Spam and DNS blocklists
  • SPF, DKIM and DMARC
  • Done-for-you fixes
  • Re-check after a fix
  • Follow-up when a scan can't finish

Price: Fix packages, one-off, in USD: TLS and redirects 69, email and DNS 89, security headers 99, SEO meta 119, accessibility 169, performance 199; or ask for a quote or book a call. After the work, a free re-review marks each finding fixed, still open or needing a person.

Sucuri SiteCheck

Remote malware scanner, with a paid cleanup and firewall platform

SiteCheck scans the public page source for malware, spam and malicious redirects, checks blocklist status with authorities such as Google and PhishTank, and flags an outdated CMS or vulnerable plugins. It sees only what a browser sees. The paid platform adds server-side scans and monitoring for malware, blocklists, DNS, uptime, malicious redirects and SEO spam.

  • Phishing and malware lists beyond Google
  • Hacked-site signs: search spam, cloaking, hidden links
  • Third-party hosts the site loads or links to
  • WordPress core, plugin and theme vulnerabilities
  • Spam and DNS blocklists
  • Done-for-you fixes
  • Re-check after a fix

Price: SiteCheck free. Platform 229, 339 or 549 USD a year per site, with unlimited malware and hack removal.

Sources, checked November 10, 2026: sitecheck.sucuri.net · sucuri.net/website-security-platform

Wordfence

WordPress security plugin, with paid hands-on plans

A plugin installed in WordPress: firewall, server-side malware scanner, and plugin and theme vulnerability monitoring. Care and Response add installation, monitoring, malware removal, blocklist removal and search engine cleanup after an incident, with after-action reports. WordPress sites only.

  • Hacked-site signs: search spam, cloaking, hidden links
  • WordPress core, plugin and theme vulnerabilities
  • Done-for-you fixes
  • Re-check after a fix

Price: Plugin free. Premium 149, Care 590, Response 1,250 USD a year per site; cleanup comes with Care and Response.

Sources, checked November 10, 2026: wordfence.com/products/pricing · wordfence.com/products/wordfence-care

MxToolbox

Mail and DNS lookups, with paid monitoring

Free lookups for MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC records and over 100 DNS blocklists, plus one Email Health check a day. Paid plans add monitoring, DMARC reports and domain impersonation protection. Managed services set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC for you.

  • Lookalike domains imitating the site
  • Spam and DNS blocklists
  • SPF, DKIM and DMARC
  • Done-for-you fixes
  • Re-check after a fix

Price: Lookups free. Delivery Center 129 USD a month, Plus 399 USD a month (5 domains); managed services by quote.

Sources, checked November 10, 2026: mxtoolbox.com/c/mxtoolboxproducts · mxtoolbox.com/emailhealth · knowledgebase.mxtoolbox.com/home/managed-services

HTTP Observatory · SecurityHeaders.com · SSL Labs

Free header and TLS graders

Each grades one layer in depth: HTTP security headers, cookies, redirects and Subresource Integrity (HTTP Observatory, SecurityHeaders.com), or the TLS setup (SSL Labs). Mozilla says the Observatory does not test outdated software or CMS plugins. You can rescan for free at any time.

  • Re-check after a fix

Price: Free.

Sources, checked November 10, 2026: developer.mozilla.org/en-US/observatory/docs/faq · securityheaders.com · ssllabs.com/ssltest

PageSpeed Insights · GTmetrix · Pingdom

Speed and quality testers

Lighthouse audits performance, accessibility, SEO and best practices; GTmetrix and Pingdom test and monitor speed and uptime. Their pages do not name threat lists, mail records or hacked-site checks. URL Review itself runs Lighthouse through PageSpeed Insights.

  • Re-check after a fix

Price: PageSpeed Insights free. GTmetrix PRO from 4.99 USD a month. Pingdom paid plans.

Sources, checked November 10, 2026: developer.chrome.com/docs/lighthouse/overview · gtmetrix.com/pricing.html · pingdom.com/pricing

Semrush · Ahrefs Site Audit

SEO crawlers

They crawl thousands of pages for 140+ (Semrush) or 170+ (Ahrefs) technical and on-page SEO issues, with scheduled re-crawls. Their pages do not name threat lists, hacked-site signs or mail records.

  • Re-check after a fix

Price: Ahrefs Site Audit free for verified sites. Semrush from 139 USD a month.

Sources, checked November 10, 2026: semrush.com/kb/31-site-audit · semrush.com/pricing · ahrefs.com/webmaster-tools

Detectify · UpGuard

Attack-surface monitoring for security teams

Continuous discovery and testing of a company's domains, subdomains and IPs. Detectify detects exposed files, misconfigurations and known vulnerabilities; UpGuard adds vulnerability detection, dark-web monitoring and, on its Standard plan, typosquatting detection. Their pages list remediation guidance, not done-for-you fixes.

  • Lookalike domains imitating the site
  • Exposed files such as .env, .git or backups
  • WordPress core, plugin and theme vulnerabilities
  • Re-check after a fix

Price: Detectify Starter from 0 EUR platform fee, scanning priced per domain or target. UpGuard from 250 USD a month; typosquatting from 19,999 USD a year.

Sources, checked November 10, 2026: detectify.com/pricing · support.detectify.com/support/solutions/articles/48001061283-what-can-i-scan-using-detectify- · help.upguard.com/en/upguard-breach-risk-pricing · help.upguard.com/en/articles/3824963-what-is-upguard-s-typosquatting-module

SiteGround · Hostinger

Scanners built into a hosting plan

Only for sites they host. SiteGround checks the site's URLs for malicious links and content and the domain against blocklists every day, scans files weekly or daily, and offers one-click cleanup on Premium. Hostinger scans files for backdoors, SEO-spam injectors and phishing kits and offers a paid one-time cleanup; it does not scan the database.

  • Phishing and malware lists beyond Google
  • Hacked-site signs: search spam, cloaking, hidden links
  • Third-party hosts the site loads or links to
  • Done-for-you fixes
  • Re-check after a fix

Price: SiteGround: paid add-on per site. Hostinger: included with Web and Cloud hosting; cleanup priced in hPanel.

Sources, checked November 10, 2026: my.siteground.com/support/kb/siteground-site-scanner · docs.hostinger.com/websites/malware-scanner
