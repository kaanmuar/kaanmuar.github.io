Fixes from review

1 · Per-asset 3D

Mark, shield, warn, fail, speed each have a unique orbit radius/inclination/period + self-spin or tumble — not short 2D bob.

2 · Background spin

Rig yaws 360° continuously (~48s). Scan plate counter-rotates. Sparks fly the outer rings. No alternate 180 flip.

3 · Hero readable

Orbit stage is behind copy (z-index) + heavier center veil. Depth fade blurs assets when they pass in front of the camera.

4 · Phrase fade

Transitions are opacity only — fade out, then fade in. No slide / 3D tilt on the headline.
