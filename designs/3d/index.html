Five 3D looks,one fusion.

Each look keeps the QA-lab palette, the Instrument Serif headlines and the crystal pyramid mark, and comes as a live hero, four social banners you can download as PNG, and a section background behind real content. Move the pointer over any of them; they respond. Fusion mixes all five and redraws the QA-lab URL Review icon in the same look.
