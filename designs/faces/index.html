Design proposals · #3826 · round one

Every check,its own face.

Today every check, section and plan shares one mark. These are three directions for giving each its own face, tried on six items: three checks, two from the new cyber-attack side and one feature. Each face is one symbol drawn two ways: an animated glass SVG that stays readable at 16 px in a report row, and a live 3D tile built from the same shape in the hero's glass. Move the pointer over a tile; it tilts. Coral and red appear only on the high and critical threat levels.

Stills and clips

Concept stills from an image model and one generated motion clip, for covers, share cards and the paid report. They are references: the shipped faces stay code (SVG and WebGL), so they keep our exact colours and shapes in six languages.

The SVG faces are self-contained files (their own CSS, no scripts) and play inside an <img>. The 3D tiles use the vendored three.js already behind the hero, one shared renderer for every tile. With reduced motion turned on, both show a still frame.
