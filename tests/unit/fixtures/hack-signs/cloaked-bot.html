Harbor Bakery

We bake sourdough loaves, croissants, cinnamon rolls and seasonal fruit tarts every morning before sunrise. Our flour comes from a family mill in the Willamette Valley, and our coffee from a roaster two streets away.

Order a birthday cake two days ahead, or stop by for a slice of lemon drizzle and a warm drink. We are open from seven in the morning until three in the afternoon, Tuesday to Sunday.
