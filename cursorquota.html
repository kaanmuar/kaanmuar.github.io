What it reads

On the Mac, the app reads Cursor’s own sign-in from Cursor’s local storage: the state database in Cursor’s Application Support folder.

It decrypts that with the “Cursor Safe Storage” item in your macOS Keychain, so macOS asks for Keychain access. It then sends that session to cursor.com to read your quota and usage.

The pairing code carries your computer’s Cursor session to your phone.

Who makes it

These apps are unofficial. They are not made by or affiliated with Cursor (Anysphere).

Cursor’s terms apply to your account. The source is private, and the apps have not had an independent security audit.

One battery, three places

Mac
A battery in the menu bar that opens at login. macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon or Intel.
Windows
A battery in the notification area. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, with Tailscale installed.
Android
Named “Cursor”. A quota bar on top, a home screen widget, and a background refresh you can allow. Android 8.0 or later.

On your phone

The Android app has two tabs. Computer shows the paired computer’s Cursor chats and a live screen view. Cloud opens cursor.com/agents.

On the Mac you can also get a notification after every agent turn, through a Cursor stop hook that runs open -g cursorquota://turn.

Allow Phone Control is optional. It asks macOS for Screen Recording and Accessibility, and while it is on, the Mac does not idle-sleep.

Pairing

Pair Phone shows a QR code on the computer, and the phone scans it.

The pairing grants access to your Cursor account and lasts until the token expires, which the app shows. Pair each phone with its own computer.

The Computer tab works from any network through a Tailscale Funnel address, or directly on the same Wi-Fi. The phone needs no VPN app.

Install

Get CursorQuota-mac.zip, CursorQuota-windows.exe or CursorQuota-android.apk from the public releases page.

  • The Mac build is not notarized. macOS says it cannot verify the developer: choose “Open Anyway” in System Settings, under Privacy & Security.
  • Windows SmartScreen may show “More info”, then “Run anyway”.
  • Android asks you to allow installing from the browser or the Files app.
  • The apps check for updates every six hours or on demand, and update themselves. The update metadata is signed: latest.json and latest.json.sig.
